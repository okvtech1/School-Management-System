Privacy Policy

We collect the information you and your organization provide directly: account details (name, email, phone, username), and whatever student/staff/financial records your organization chooses to store in the system.

  • What it's used for: operating your account, sending login/reset emails, and — only if you contact us — responding to support requests.
  • Who can see it: only your organization's own users, according to the roles you assign them. Each organization's data lives in its own private spreadsheet — no other organization can access it.
  • Third parties: email delivery (Gmail/Google Workspace), and, if your organization enables them, SMS/WhatsApp gateways or payment processors you configure yourself.
  • Your rights: you can request a copy or deletion of your organization's data at any time by contacting the platform owner.
