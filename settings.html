School Info & Current Term

These appear on ID cards, result sheets, clearance lists, and the public result checker — and drive which term's scores are being entered right now.

Branding & Printouts

Your logo appears at the top-left of every printout (result sheets, passcodes, clearance lists, ID cards, reports, timetables and receipts) in your school's own colours.
Main colour: headings, table headers and the letterhead rule. Accent: the thin second rule and highlights.

Public Portal — School Bank Account (for Fee Payment)

Shown to parents on the public Pay Fees page before they make a bank transfer, so they know exactly where to send the money before uploading their receipt.
This is the school's own account for collecting fees from parents — separate from OKV Technology's account on the Upgrade page, which is for your subscription payment.

Term Info — current term/session

These print on every result sheet for the term/session selected above (Settings → School Info).

Schools / Branches

Running more than one school or campus under this account? Add each one here, then pick the active one — Students, Staff, Classes, and Results are all filtered to whichever school is active. Leave this empty if you only run a single school.

Classes & Arms

Add each class your school runs, choose its section (this decides which CA count applies), then list its arms (e.g. A, B, C, D) separated by commas. Leave arms blank for a class with no arms.

Subjects

Each section has its own subject list — Nursery subjects (e.g. Writing, Poem, Drawing/Colouring) are usually very different from Secondary subjects (e.g. Physics, Chemistry). Pick a section below to manage its list.

Continuous Assessment (CA) Count

How many CA scores are entered per term before the exam score, by section.

Grading Method

Choose the grade scale used on score entry, report sheets, result checks and dashboards. Existing scores are regraded automatically.

Principal / Head Teacher Signature

This signature will automatically appear on result sheets, clearance slips, and other official documents wherever a principal's signature is needed.

Attendance & Data Management

Attendance is always recorded per class per day and rolled up into "days present" totals per student automatically — this only controls how long the day-by-day records stay in your live spreadsheet before moving to your Archive (nothing is ever deleted).
Email sending has a daily limit shared across every school on this platform. If that limit is reached on a given day, remaining emails for that run are skipped (never duplicated later) — this is rare, but worth knowing before enabling it for a very large school.
When set, every parent gets a one-time term attendance summary automatically on this date (or the next day the system checks, if the school was offline that day). Leave blank to skip automatic term summaries.
Fee amounts shown on result sheets and the public payment page come from Fee Payments → Fee Types, not from here.
