Your daily routine

  1. Overview: look at Schools Needing Attention (trials ending within 3 days, renewals due within 7 days, expired or cancelled accounts) and message those schools.
  2. Upgrade Approvals: open each request, check the payment screenshot against your bank, then Approve (the plan, start and due dates are set and the school is emailed) or Reject (the school is emailed to get in touch).
  3. Schools: search a school, edit its contact, plan, billing cycle, status or dates, and see its user count and capacity health.
  4. Messaging: email all schools or one school. SMS and WhatsApp send only once you connect a gateway.

Platform Config

Set your platform name, colours and logo (they appear live on the sales pages), the bank details shown on the Upgrade page, and the keys for email, SMS and WhatsApp. Payment gateway keys can be stored, but online card checkout is not part of this release, so upgrades are confirmed from a bank transfer.

Keeping the system healthy

  • Run setupCapacityMonitorTrigger, setupArchiveTrigger, setupWeeklyAttendanceEmailTrigger and setupTermSummaryEmailTrigger once from the Apps Script editor.
  • runMaintenanceNow() trims empty spreadsheet space, archives old records for every school and refreshes the capacity report. It is safe to run any time.
  • findDuplicateAdmissionNumbers() lists any admission numbers shared by more than one student in schools that existed before the unique-number rule.
  • A school flagged Monitor or Action Needed in capacity should be archived (run maintenance) before anything else.
  • After editing Code.gs or Tenancy.gs, deploy a new version: Deploy, Manage deployments, Edit, New version.
