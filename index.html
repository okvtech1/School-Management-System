About the System

OKV School Management System is a complete, multi-tenant platform built for schools — from nursery through secondary. One login for Admins, Principals, Teachers, Bursars, and Exam Officers; one shareable link for students and parents to check results, sit CBT exams, and pay fees. Every school's data is fully isolated — your records are never visible to another school on the platform.

Built for real school workflows

Results with auto-generated remarks, ID cards, exam clearance, timetables — modeled on how schools actually run, not a generic spreadsheet.

Your data, isolated and safe

Every school operates in its own private space. Role-based access means staff only ever see what their role permits.

Always up to date

Changes sync automatically across every device in your school, with a manual sync option whenever you want to be sure.
